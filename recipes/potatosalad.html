Description

This creamy Dominican potato salad combines tender potatoes, carrots, and red beets in a tangy mayonnaise dressing. Its bright pink color makes it a beautiful side for any meal.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 2 medium red beets
  • 2 medium carrots, peeled and diced
  • 3 hard-boiled eggs, chopped
  • ½ small red onion, finely diced
  • ½ cup mayonnaise
  • 1 tablespoon white vinegar
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Cook beets: Boil whole, unpeeled beets for 35–50 minutes, until tender. Cool, peel, and dice.
  2. Cook potatoes and carrots: Boil potatoes in salted water for 10–15 minutes. Add carrots during the last 8 minutes. Drain when tender.
  3. Cool: Let the vegetables cool completely before mixing.
  4. Make dressing: Mix mayonnaise, vinegar, salt, and pepper in a large bowl.
  5. Combine: Gently stir in potatoes, carrots, beets, eggs, and onion.
  6. Chill: Refrigerate for at least 1 hour. Serve cold.
